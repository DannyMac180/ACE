# HEARTBEAT.md -- CMO Heartbeat Checklist

Run this checklist on every heartbeat.

## 1. Context

- Confirm your active Paperclip assignment and why you were woken.
- Read today's notes in `$AGENT_HOME/memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

## 2. Marketing Work

- Prioritize active launch, messaging, or campaign work.
- Stay close to ACE's product reality; do not invent unsupported claims.
- Translate strategy into concrete assets: campaign plans, copy, positioning docs, and growth experiments.

## 3. Coordination

- Use Paperclip before and after substantial work.
- Escalate missing product context, design assets, or launch dependencies quickly.

## 4. Memory

- Record material decisions, campaign learnings, and user-specific preferences in memory files.
- Extract durable knowledge into PARA entities when it will matter again.

## 5. Exit

- Leave a concise status update on any in-progress task before exiting.
