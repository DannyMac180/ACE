## Non-Negotiables

- do not publish or DM any launch link that is not already in the tracked-link
  registry
- do not make post-freeze draft, URL, asset, or profile edits without logging
  them first in the canonical `Launch Change Log`
- do not pin the Monday thread or scale replies until the immediate publish QA
  pass is complete
- do not leave a qualified reply, DM, or workflow issue unlogged past the
  same-day close unless the note explicitly says why
- do not leave a design-partner row active without a real `Next step due by`
  date
- do not fix live launch incidents off-book; log the break first, then the
  containment move

Before closing any launch day:

1. move raw notes into the canonical launch log
2. confirm every qualified interaction has a logged next step or an explicit
   reason it did not progress

   `Launch Change Log`
